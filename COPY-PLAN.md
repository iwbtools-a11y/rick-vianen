## Key Changes vs. Current Site

### Language
- **Current**: Mix of English and Dutch, generic coaching language
- **New**: 100% Dutch, Rick's voice — direct, confronterend, energiek. First person (ik/jij). Never "u".

### Pricing (WRONG on current site)
- **Current**: Core €499/mo, Pro €849/mo, Elite €1,800/mo (monthly)
- **Correct**: MOVE Core €1.497/kw, MOVE Plus €2.497/kw, MOVE Ultimate €4.997/kw (per kwartaal / 13 weken)

### Method
- **Current**: Generic "Audit, Architectuur, Actie, Autoriteit"
- **Correct**: MOVE methode — Master, Operate, Validate, Elevate (wekelijkse cyclus)

### Target Audience
- **Current**: Generic "high-performers"
- **Correct**: Two ICPs: (1) Vastgelopen Ondernemer 35-50, (2) Ambitieuze Professional 30-45

### Tone
- **Current**: Slick English marketing speak
- **Correct**: Informeel, confronterend, kort. "Kan mijn lezer 'Nou en...' denken? Zo ja -> schrappen."

## Tone of Voice Checklist (apply everywhere)

- [ ] All copy in Dutch (remove ALL English)
- [ ] First person (ik/jij), never "u"
- [ ] No forbidden words: transformationele reis, manifesteren, holistisch, synergie, authentiek
- [ ] No fake urgency: "Limited spots!" "Nu of nooit!" — use real scarcity (8 plekken per cohort)
- [ ] Every section passes the "Nou en..." test
- [ ] Open from reader's perspective, not Rick's achievements
- [ ] Use real client quotes where possible
- [ ] Short sentences. White space. High tempo.
- [ ] Confronting but from connection, not judgment
